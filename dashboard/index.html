Concentration over time

The Nakamoto coefficient is how many computor slots must act together to control one third of revenue — lower means more concentrated. Gini measures how unevenly revenue is spread across slots (0 = even, 1 = one slot takes all). Both count slots; where one operator holds several, the true figures are lower.
