Qubic runs on 676 computor slots. On paper that looks like 676 independent operators — but a single operator can hold many slots at once. This report groups slots by operator (self-reported first, on-chain-linked next) and measures how concentrated revenue and control really are.

Operator map

Each tile is one operator; area = share of the epoch. Declared operators are self-reported; “unattributed” are slots with no proven owner.
