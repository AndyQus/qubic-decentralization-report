About this page

Since epoch 240 Qubic's mining is graded on the real direction of Bitcoin: every hour the nodes store the BTC/USDT price on-chain through Qubic's Price oracle, and the ant colony searches for networks whose output matches up and down. Here is every hour as it arrives.

Directions only, no prices: the prices come from Binance via Qubic's Price oracle, and this report does not republish exchange data. Times in your local time zone. Not investment advice — a research experiment of the Qubic network.

How the calls are made

The output of the colony's best network for that hour.

Each call is fixed as soon as the hour before it is on-chain.
